Privacy Policy for Flappy Chuppy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

Flappy Chuppy (package name com.vibecode.flappychuppy) is developed and operated by Hùng Lê. This Privacy Policy explains how the game handles information.

Information stored on your device

The game stores gameplay progress, completed levels, best scores and times, recent run history, sound preference, and selected scene mode locally on your device. This information is used only to provide game functionality. It is not sent to Hùng Lê's servers because the game does not provide user accounts or an online leaderboard.

Advertising

The production version of the game uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display banner and interstitial advertising. Google and its advertising partners may collect or receive information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, app interaction information, and approximate location inferred from IP address in order to provide, measure, personalize where permitted, and prevent fraud in advertising.

This information is processed by Google under its own policies. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and Advertising Technologies information.

Sharing

The game can open Android's system share sheet when you choose to share a completed-level result. The game only prepares the result text; it does not choose a recipient or post content on your behalf. Any information shared is governed by the service you select.

Data retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the game. You can delete it at any time through Android Settings > Apps > Flappy Chuppy > Storage > Clear data, or by uninstalling the app. Advertising-related data is retained by Google according to Google's policies.

Children

Flappy Chuppy does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Because the game uses third-party advertising, parents or guardians should review Google's advertising privacy information before allowing a child to use the game.

Security

We do not operate a server that receives gameplay data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we limit local data to what is needed for the game.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game or its data practices change. The current version will be published with a revised effective date.
